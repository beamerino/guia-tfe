# Checklist

Tick the boxes as you go. **Your progress is saved in this browser**, so you can close the page and come back later. Nothing is sent anywhere: nobody else sees what you tick.

## Before you start writing

<div class="checklist" data-id="inicio" markdown>

- [ ] I have **a single main objective**, in one sentence, with a verb that can be checked.
- [ ] I've broken the main objective into **2–4 secondary objectives**.
- [ ] I've passed the [feasibility test](tema.md#feasibility-test): time, data, tools and evaluation.
- [ ] I know which **type of literature review** I'm doing and which **framework** I'm following (JBI, PRISMA…).
- [ ] I've searched **at least two academic databases** and documented the searches.
- [ ] I have a **reference manager** installed and I'm using it.
- [ ] I've checked whether my project fits a **reporting guideline** (EQUATOR).
- [ ] If I work with data about people, I've sorted out permissions and, if needed, the **ethics committee**.
- [ ] I have a **timeline** with the submission date and slack for reviews.
- [ ] I've downloaded a **template** with the thesis structure.

</div>

## Before you submit your thesis

<div class="checklist" data-id="entrega" markdown>

- [ ] The thesis follows the structure: introduction, related work, objectives, materials and methods, results, discussion, conclusions and references.
- [ ] **Every secondary objective** has its step in the method and **its result**.
- [ ] The related work ends by explaining **the gap** my project fills.
- [ ] The methods say **which methodology** I followed and allow the work to be repeated.
- [ ] The results present facts only; interpretation is in the **discussion**.
- [ ] The discussion **compares my results with the state of the art** and includes the **limitations**.
- [ ] There is **one conclusion per objective**, plus future work.
- [ ] The **abstract** includes objective, methods, results with figures, and conclusion.
- [ ] All **references** are generated with the manager, in a single style, and I've checked them one by one.
- [ ] Every citation in the text is in the reference list, and vice versa.
- [ ] All **figures and tables** are numbered, have captions and are mentioned in the text.
- [ ] All **acronyms** are defined the first time they appear.
- [ ] I've **declared any use of generative AI**.
- [ ] The table of contents and cross-references are **up to date** (no "??" or "Error! Reference source not found").
- [ ] I've reread the whole thesis **aloud or after letting it rest**.
- [ ] The document meets **my university's regulations**: length, cover page, format and any extra paperwork.
- [ ] I've sent the draft to my supervisor **with enough time to review it**.

</div>

## Before your defence

<div class="checklist" data-id="defensa" markdown>

- [ ] I know **how long** the presentation and the question time are, and whether it's in person or online.
- [ ] The presentation follows the thesis structure, with **about one slide per minute**.
- [ ] Each slide has **a title that states the idea**, and the slides are **numbered**.
- [ ] I've **rehearsed aloud with a timer** at least three times and **I don't run over**.
- [ ] I've rehearsed **in front of someone**.
- [ ] I have answers ready for the **usual committee questions**.
- [ ] I have **backup slides** for details I might be asked about.
- [ ] If there's a demo, I'm also bringing a **recorded video**.
- [ ] I have the presentation **as a PDF and in its original format**, in two different places.
- [ ] I've tested the **laptop, projector or connection** (if online) before starting.

</div>
