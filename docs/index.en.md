<section class="bloque">
  <p class="etiqueta"><span class="guion"></span>What I repeat most</p>
  <h2>Three ideas that change everything</h2>
  <div class="ideas">
    <a class="idea" href="tema/">
      <span class="idea-num">1</span>
      <h3>One focused objective beats five ambitious ones</h3>
      <p>The most common mistake is trying to do too much. Choose one main objective you can genuinely achieve with your time, data and tools.</p>
    </a>
    <a class="idea" href="estado-del-arte/">
      <span class="idea-num">2</span>
      <h3>A literature review needs a method</h3>
      <p>Systematic, scoping or narrative review; frameworks such as JBI or PRISMA; databases such as Web of Science, Scopus or PubMed. A Google search won't do.</p>
    </a>
    <a class="idea" href="estructura/">
      <span class="idea-num">3</span>
      <h3>One structure works almost every time</h3>
      <p>Plus a simple rule to make everything fit: each secondary objective is a method step, and each step produces one result.</p>
    </a>
  </div>
</section>
